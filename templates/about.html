About the publication

Outside the Headlines

For readers who follow the news and are bored with its repeated choices about what matters.

We look for consequential developments in the long tail: changes in institutions, science, law, markets and ordinary life that deserve attention beyond the places where they happened. An unfamiliar location is not, by itself, a reason to include a story.

Each edition is a finite collection, selected and edited as a whole. When several developments support a genuinely useful inference, a Pattern brings them together. Humor is optional.

Sources and corrections

Summaries link to the original reporting. Each story has a compact evidence disclosure for readers who want to check a particular claim. Reporting and source material remain the property of their respective publishers.

Material corrections are dated on the issue page. An edition’s original date and publication timestamp remain visible when it is revised.

An editorial experiment

Software and language models help with research and drafting; an editor is responsible for selection, factual checking and the final copy. This is an early publication experiment. Its novelty, reading value and appeal have not yet been established through reader testing.
