About the publication

Outside the Headlines

For readers who follow the news and want to read outside the repeated and narrow confines of the headlines.

We look for consequential developments in the long tail: changes in institutions, science, law, markets and ordinary life.

Our back story

There used to be a time when people received their paper newspaper in the morning, and in the best cases had the time to sip a coffee and read the back pages, where you sometimes stumbled upon obscure news stories about places you'd never heard of, or dynamics that you hadn't considered. And in a few of these cases, you learned more than you would have learned from reading a hundred more headlines.

These days we tend to doomscroll or read the latest headlines in a news app, which are most likely very similar to yesterday's headlines. I wanted to be able to recreate the discovery and depth of the back pages of a newspaper by leveraging AI's ability to search the depths of the internet, while at the same time including a human editorial review process that focuses on articles that bring insight to how the world is changing and dynamics that are not captured by the headlines and front pages.

Sources and corrections

Summaries link to the original reporting. Each story has a compact evidence disclosure for readers who want to check a particular claim. Reporting and source material remain the property of their respective publishers.

Material corrections are dated on the issue page. An edition’s original date and publication timestamp remain visible when it is revised.

An editorial experiment

Software and language models help with research and drafting; an editor is responsible for selection, presentation, and overall direction. This is an early publication experiment.
